Loneliness and first-generation burnout counselling in Vancouver.

Unravel Counselling offers warm, culturally responsive virtual counselling for South Asian and first-generation immigrant women across British Columbia. Founded by Theekshitha Vadladi, a South Asian counsellor in her late 20s, the practice supports women who are tired of feeling alone in a crowded city, holding everyone else together, or living between two worlds.

Therapy can help with the Vancouver Freeze, eldest daughter burnout, cultural isolation, guilt of success, and family boundaries that do not require going no contact. Using ACT, EMDR, and somatic and relational approaches, sessions create space to process what you carry and move toward a life aligned with your own values.
