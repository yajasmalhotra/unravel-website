THE PERSON BEHIND UNRAVEL

Hi, I’m Theekshitha.

I’m a mental health counsellor focused on OCD and anxiety. I want to understand your life as well as your symptoms. My background as a South Asian woman and first-generation immigrant is part of why I work that way. We’ll look at what keeps drawing you into worry or rituals, while taking your relationships, beliefs and responsibilities into account.

WHAT I CAN HELP WITH

When OCD or anxiety takes up too much of your day.

OCD COUNSELLING

When one more check never feels like enough.

Checking, asking for reassurance or mentally reviewing can bring relief, then leave you back with the same doubt. I use ERP to work on these compulsive responses, with ACT supporting the work.

Explore OCD counselling

ANXIETY COUNSELLING

When worry keeps deciding what you do.

You might overprepare, put off decisions or avoid things you want to take part in. ACT-based counselling looks at those responses alongside the real pressures you are living with.

Explore anxiety counselling

WORKING TOGETHER

What changes between appointments matters.

Counselling might focus on finishing a task without prolonged checking, making a decision with some uncertainty, or being more present with someone you care about.

We’ll discuss what to practise and review what happens in your week. If something isn’t helping, we’ll look at why and what needs to change.

PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Before you book.

Who are sessions for?

Unravel offers virtual counselling to adults aged 18 and over who are located in British Columbia at the time of care, including Vancouver.

What does counselling cost?

Individual sessions are CAD $145 for 50 minutes; extended sessions are CAD $190 for 90 minutes. Limited reduced-fee places may be available. Explore fees.

What happens in the consultation?

We talk for 20 minutes by phone or video about what you want help with, how I might work with you and practical questions. The consultation is free.

YOUR NEXT STEP

Begin with a conversation.

Tell me what you are looking for, ask your questions and hear how I might work with you. You can take time afterwards to decide.
