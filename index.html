HOW WE TREAT

The work continues beyond the hour.

Therapy is a guided process, shaped around your goals and the life you want to inhabit.

Before sessions, we make room to understand what matters and choose a direction together. In sessions, EMDR, ERP or ACT can offer different ways to work with patterns that have felt fixed.

Between sessions, reflection and practice help carry insight into everyday choices, relationships and moments of possibility.
