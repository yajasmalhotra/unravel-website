MORE ROOM TO LIVE.

WHAT WE TREAT

OCD and anxiety counselling.

These difficulties can overlap, but the patterns deserve careful attention. Explore the care that best describes what you are experiencing.

01

Obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD)

↗

OCD can involve intrusive thoughts, checking, reassurance-seeking and rituals that happen entirely in your mind. Care uses ERP to address the compulsive response, within an ACT-led framework focused on the life OCD is interrupting.

Explore OCD therapy ↗
02

Anxiety

↗

Persistent worry can leave you tense, overprepared or avoiding decisions. ACT-based anxiety counselling examines those responses and develops more flexible ways to act, with attention to the real pressures you face.

Explore anxiety counselling ↗

HOW WE TREAT

Therapy that connects with everyday life.

Therapy needs a purpose you recognise: time returned from rituals, a decision you can make, or fuller participation in the relationships and activities you value.

Early sessions explore what sets the difficulty in motion, what happens next and how that response affects your day. Your goals and circumstances help shape a plan, with a clear explanation of the approach being proposed.

For OCD, the work includes ERP: planned practice with triggers while changing compulsive responses. ACT connects the work with values and psychological flexibility. Agreed practice and review help carry learning into everyday life.

PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Practical details, clearly stated.

Who are sessions for?

Unravel offers virtual counselling to adults aged 18 and over who are located in British Columbia at the time of care, including Vancouver.

What does counselling cost?

Individual sessions are CAD $145 for 50 minutes; extended sessions are CAD $190 for 90 minutes. Limited reduced-fee places may be available. Explore fees.

How can I explore the fit?

A free 15-minute phone or video consultation covers the approach, fees and availability. It is an introduction before deciding on paid sessions.

FOR SOMEONE YOU CARE ABOUT

Supporting someone you care about.

When someone you love is struggling with OCD or anxiety, it can be difficult to know how to help. Sharing clear information about care can be a gentle way to open a conversation.

Listening, respecting their privacy and leaving the choice to begin with them can support that conversation. This website explains individual counselling, the approaches used and how to ask practical questions.
