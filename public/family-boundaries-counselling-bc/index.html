Family boundaries counselling in British Columbia

More space for yourself does not have to mean less love for your family.

Culturally responsive counselling for women who want clearer boundaries without going no contact, detonating family relationships, or carrying constant guilt.

Let’s unravel this togetherMeet Theekshitha

Boundary guilt can be louder than your own needs.

You may know what you want to say, then hear your mother’s disappointment, your father’s worry, or an entire family story about what a “good daughter” does. You might share too much because privacy feels rude, say yes when you mean no, or avoid important conversations until resentment takes over. When you live between two worlds, boundaries can feel like a threat to belonging.

Therapy can help you define what is yours to carry and what is not, without reducing your family to villains or asking you to choose between independence and connection. We can practise language for limits, prepare for pushback, and build the nervous-system steadiness to follow through. The goal is not perfect boundaries. It is more choice, less fear, and relationships that have room for the real you.

Boundaries rooted in values, not punishment

ACT can help you make decisions based on your values even when guilt is present. Somatic and relational work can help you notice when your body goes into appeasing, freezing, or over-explaining. EMDR may support the deeper experiences that make differentiation feel unsafe.

We may work on

People-pleasing, privacy, visits, money, caregiving, marriage pressure, conflict, and saying no without over-explaining.

Family loyalty and your autonomy can both matter. You do not have to pick one to be supported here.
