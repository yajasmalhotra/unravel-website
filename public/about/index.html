About Unravel Counselling.

Online OCD and anxiety counselling for adults in Vancouver and across British Columbia. Focused clinical care, with room for the culture, relationships and responsibilities that shape your life.

Book a free consultation

Understand the pattern.
Keep the person in view.

OCD and anxiety can make an ordinary day demanding. Time goes into checking, rehearsing, avoiding or trying to feel certain. From the outside, you may seem to be managing; inside, the effort can be considerable.

Unravel was founded by Theekshitha Vadladi to offer care that connects those patterns with the life around them. Understanding what keeps a difficulty going matters. So do the family commitments, beliefs, pressures and possibilities that shape what a useful change would look like.

The practice focuses on OCD and anxiety. Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) is the main framework, with Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP) for OCD. EMDR is a secondary approach for anxiety and trauma where appropriate.

Culture belongs in the work.

Unravel brings an understanding of South Asian and first-generation experiences and welcomes adults from all backgrounds. Family connection, faith and belonging can be sources of strength as well as places where difficult expectations arise. Their meaning is yours to explain.

Context

Migration, discrimination, finances and caring responsibilities affect what is possible. They deserve attention alongside symptoms and coping patterns.

Collaboration

The purpose of an approach should be clear. Your questions, priorities and choices shape the plan and how it is reviewed.

Practical change

The work connects with everyday life: how your time is spent, what you avoid and what you want to participate in more fully.

What the name means.

To unravel something is to understand how its threads became tangled. In therapy, that can mean noticing a cycle of doubt and rituals, a familiar response to worry, or an expectation that has become difficult to carry.

Change can involve loosening those patterns while keeping the commitments that matter. The direction is personal: being more present with someone you love, completing a task without prolonged checking, or making a decision without waiting for complete confidence.

Values that fit your life.

ACT asks what you want your actions to stand for. That question is understood within your culture, relationships and responsibilities. Connection with family and a wish for change can belong in the same conversation.

In OCD care, ERP addresses the compulsive responses that restrict your choices. Values help give that practice a purpose beyond finishing an exercise. For anxiety, the work considers how to act meaningfully when worry is present.

A clearer starting point.

Sessions are virtual for adults aged 18 and over who are physically in BC. A free 15-minute phone or video consultation covers the approach, availability and practical questions.
