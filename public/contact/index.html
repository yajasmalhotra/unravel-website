Start here

Start with a simple conversation.

To ask about virtual counselling, a consultation, current fees, or availability, email Unravel Counselling. The practice serves adults who are located in British Columbia at the time of care.

Email Unravel CounsellingOur philosophy

What to include

You are welcome to share your name, that you are located in British Columbia, and a little about what you are looking for. You do not need to provide private clinical details in an initial email. Unravel Counselling will reply with practical information about next steps, including current availability and whether a consultation may be a helpful place to begin.

Privacy and urgent support

Email is not a crisis or emergency service and should not be used for urgent support or highly sensitive health information. If you are in immediate danger or need urgent help, call 911 or contact a local crisis service. Please read the website privacy notice for information about enquiries and privacy questions.
