WHAT WE CAN TALK ABOUT

Bring the questions you want answered.

We can talk about the support you are seeking, how I might work with you, and questions about appointments or fees. You can take time after the call to decide whether you would like to continue.

Read what happens in the consultation and first appointments.

IF YOU DECIDE TO CONTINUE

What happens after the consultation?

If working together seems appropriate and you choose to continue, we arrange an individual appointment and go through the intake information. Early sessions explore what is affecting your life and the goals that will guide the work. Read about the first counselling sessions.

PRIVACY & URGENT SUPPORT

A note about initial enquiries.

Email is not an emergency or crisis service and should not be used for urgent support. If you are in immediate danger or need urgent help, call 911 or contact a local crisis service.
