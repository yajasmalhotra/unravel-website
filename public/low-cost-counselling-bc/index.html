FEES & FORMAT

Counselling fees and reduced-fee options.

Individual online OCD and anxiety counselling for adults in Vancouver and across British Columbia. Review session lengths and prices before choosing your next step.

Reduced-fee counselling, subject to availability.

If the standard fee is a barrier, ask about reduced-fee places during the consultation. These places are limited; availability and any arrangement need to be confirmed individually.

The free consultation is also a chance to understand the approach and practical arrangements before paying for a session. There is no need to include private clinical details in an email about fees.

Questions about paying for counselling.

Are OCD or ERP sessions charged differently?

The individual rates above apply to OCD counselling, including ERP, and anxiety counselling. The method and appropriate session length are considered in relation to your needs.

Will insurance cover my sessions?

Coverage varies by plan. Before booking a paid session, confirm with your insurer whether your plan covers sessions with a pre-licensed counsellor. Contact Unravel if you need provider information to share with your insurer.

What is included in the free consultation?

A 15-minute conversation by phone or video about fit, the approach, fees and availability. It is an introduction rather than a full counselling session or formal diagnostic assessment.

How many sessions should I budget for?

There is no fixed course promised. Goals, frequency, affordability and the usefulness of the work are reviewed as counselling continues. Ask about how progress and next steps would be considered.

Where can I check payment and cancellation details?

Ask about payment arrangements, receipts and cancellation terms before your first paid appointment. Contact Unravel with practical questions.

Know the details before beginning.

Book a free 15-minute phone or video consultation to ask about care and current reduced-fee availability. Sessions are virtual for adults aged 18+ physically located in BC.
