Low cost counselling · virtual across BC

Support should feel more possible to reach.

Cost can be a real barrier to beginning therapy. Unravel Counselling offers a place to ask about current low cost options, fees, and availability without pressure.

Ask about options ↗

A clear conversation about practical details.

You do not have to have everything figured out before reaching out. If cost is shaping your decision, you are welcome to ask about current low cost counselling availability, session fees, and whether there may be an option that fits your circumstances.

Availability can change, so practical details are shared directly rather than promised on the website. A brief enquiry is enough to begin; there is no need to share private clinical details by email.
