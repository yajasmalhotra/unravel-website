ACCESS TO COUNSELLING

Low-cost or sliding-scale counselling.

If the standard fee is a barrier, ask about reduced-fee places during the consultation. Places are limited, and availability and the fee need to be agreed individually.

We can discuss session length and frequency alongside the work you want help with and the budget you can sustain. The free consultation gives you a chance to ask about those arrangements before paying for a session.

INSURANCE & PAYMENT

Check your plan’s provider requirements.

Coverage depends on your policy. I practise as a pre-licensed counsellor, so please check whether your plan covers sessions with a provider at my current professional status before booking paid appointments.

If your insurer needs information about my background, contact me and I can help you identify the details to ask about. Payment arrangements, receipts and cancellation terms can be discussed before the first paid session.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Planning the practical side.

Are OCD or ERP appointments charged differently?

No. The individual rates apply: CAD $145 for 50 minutes or CAD $190 for a 90-minute extended appointment.

What is included in the free consultation?

A 20-minute phone or video conversation about what you want help with, how I might work with you, and practical questions. Read about the consultation.

How many sessions should I budget for?

We discuss the work involved and a possible frequency, then review the plan as counselling continues. The number of sessions depends on your needs, goals and how the work is progressing.

Can I attend from outside Vancouver?

Yes. Appointments are online for adults aged 18 and over who are physically located in British Columbia during sessions.
