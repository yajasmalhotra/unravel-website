How to begin ↗
MORE ROOM TO LIVE.

A LIFE THAT FEELS LIKE YOURS

Care that begins with your experience.

Acceptance and commitment therapy, or ACT, helps people relate to thoughts and feelings with greater flexibility. The work can include mindfulness, acceptance, clarifying values, and taking meaningful action.

Rather than waiting for every difficult feeling to disappear, you can explore how to make choices that reflect the life you want to build.

THE PROCESS

What care can look like.

A direction shaped together, with support before, during, and between sessions.

01

Name what matters

Explore the relationships, directions, and everyday moments you want to make room for.

02

Notice with more flexibility

Practise relating to difficult thoughts and feelings without letting them make every decision.

03

Take meaningful steps

Choose small actions that connect the work in session to life outside it.

A GOOD QUESTION

Do I need to feel ready before I start?

You can begin by talking about what matters to you and what has been getting in the way. The work can take shape from there.
