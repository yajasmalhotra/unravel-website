Couples therapy is a shared space to slow down recurring patterns, hear what sits beneath conflict, and make room for each partner’s needs and history.

Book a consultation

Conflict is often a pattern, not a character flaw.

When tension keeps returning, it can be easy to focus on who is right, who cares more, or who has failed to change. Therapy can help both partners notice the cycle they get pulled into and understand what each person is protecting, reaching for, or afraid to lose.

We can explore communication, repair, intimacy, family expectations, cultural differences, trust, and changes that have put pressure on the relationship. The goal is not perfect agreement. It is a more honest, respectful way of being on the same team.
