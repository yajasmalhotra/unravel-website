When every conversation turns into the same fight.

You may argue about dishes, money, sex, family, or whose turn it is to apologize. But underneath the topic, the same cycle keeps showing up—and both of you are tired of losing to it.

Book a consultation

The problem is not always the argument. It is what takes over during it.

One person pushes for an answer while the other shuts down. A small criticism becomes proof that nothing has changed. By the end, you are debating who started it instead of talking about what either of you actually needed.

I am not there to keep score or decide which partner is “the problem.” Together, we will slow the cycle down, understand what each of you is protecting, and practise saying the difficult thing before it comes out as blame, distance, or another familiar fight.

Catch the cycle earlier

We will identify the moment a conversation stops being about the issue and starts becoming a threat to the relationship.

Make room for two realities

Your needs, values, family histories, and cultural expectations can be different without one person having to be wrong.

Practise repair that actually lands

We will build clearer language for accountability, boundaries, closeness, and the conversations you keep postponing.
