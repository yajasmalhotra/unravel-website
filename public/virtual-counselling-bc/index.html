Virtual counselling in British Columbia

Support that meets you where you are.

Unravel Counselling provides virtual counselling for adults located throughout British Columbia. Sessions take place by secure video, making it possible to access care from a space that feels private and workable for you.

Book a consultationFees and booking

Online, but still personal.

Virtual counselling can offer the same space for reflection, care, and change without needing to travel to an office. You can join from home or another private location in British Columbia, using a device with a camera, sound, and a reliable internet connection.

The work stays collaborative: we begin with what is present for you, make sense of patterns that may no longer fit, and move at a pace that supports safety and choice.

Who it may support

Adults navigating anxiety, life transitions, identity, relationships, self-worth, cultural stress, and emotional overwhelm.

How sessions work

After booking, you will receive the current session details and a secure video link. A consultation can help determine whether the practice is a good fit.

Where it is available

Virtual sessions are available to clients who are located in British Columbia at the time of care.
