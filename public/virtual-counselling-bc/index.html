Virtual counselling in British Columbia

Support for the life you are building between two worlds.

Unravel Counselling provides virtual counselling across BC for South Asian and first-generation immigrant women navigating loneliness, eldest daughter burnout, cultural isolation, and family boundaries.

Let’s unravel this togetherFees and booking

Online, but still personal.

Virtual counselling can offer a private place to be honest about the Vancouver Freeze, the guilt of success, or the family boundary you are afraid to set. You can join from home or another private location in British Columbia.

The work stays collaborative and practical. We begin with what is present, make sense of patterns that may no longer fit, and use ACT, EMDR, and somatic and relational approaches at a pace that supports safety and choice.

Who it may support

South Asian and first-generation immigrant women navigating loneliness, burnout, cultural stress, identity, relationships, and emotional overwhelm.

How sessions work

After booking, you will receive the current session details and a secure video link. A consultation can help determine whether the practice is a good fit.

Where it is available

Virtual sessions are available to clients who are located in British Columbia at the time of care.
