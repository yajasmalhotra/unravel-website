Healing should not mean leaving yourself behind.

Unravel Counselling exists for people who have learned to carry a great deal—family expectation, cultural responsibility, achievement, care for others, and the quiet work of making every room feel comfortable.

Book a free consultation

What is tangled can be worked loose.

For many South Asian and first-generation clients, therapy can feel like a choice between feeling better and keeping the people, values, and culture that made them who they are. Unravel begins from a different place. We can make room for what is painful without reducing your story to a problem to solve.

The work is collaborative and paced. We get curious about the patterns that protect you, the expectations that exhaust you, and the parts of life you want to keep. There is no requirement to become less caring, less connected, or less yourself in order to have more space to breathe.

Why this practice exists.

Theekshitha created Unravel Counselling to offer a space where nuance is welcome. Family love and family pressure can coexist. Success can bring pride and guilt. Boundaries can be an act of care rather than a rejection of where you come from.

Drawing from person-centred, ACT-informed, trauma-informed, somatic, relational, and EMDR approaches, sessions are shaped around your goals, pace, and nervous system. The aim is not to undo who you are. It is to help you carry less of what is no longer yours alone to hold.
