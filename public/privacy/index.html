PRIVACY & CARE

Your privacy matters.

This notice explains how Unravel Counselling handles information sent through this website or by initial email enquiry. Last updated November 2, 2026.

Contact Unravel CounsellingAsk a privacy question

Information in an enquiry

When you email Unravel Counselling, you may choose to provide your name, email address, and the contents of your message. This information is used to reply to your enquiry, answer practical questions, and arrange a consultation if appropriate. Please do not include urgent or highly sensitive health information in an initial email. Email is not monitored as a crisis or emergency service.

Use and disclosure

Unravel Counselling does not sell personal information. Information from an initial enquiry is used only for the purpose for which it was provided, except where disclosure is required or permitted by law. If you become a client, privacy, consent, and record-handling information will be discussed through the practice’s intake process.

Booking links and privacy questions

Booking is provided through Jane. Jane and other linked websites or services have their own privacy practices. For a privacy question, or to request access to or correction of personal information you have provided to Unravel Counselling, email theekshitha@unravelcounselling.com. This notice may be updated as the website or practice changes.
