MORE ROOM TO LIVE.

MAKE ROOM FOR CHOICE

Care that begins with your experience.

Exposure and response prevention, or ERP, is a structured approach often used for OCD. It involves gradually approaching feared situations or thoughts while practising a response that does not rely on compulsions or rituals.

The work is planned together. Your counsellor helps you choose steps that connect to your goals, reflect your circumstances, and can be carried into everyday life.

THE PROCESS

What care can look like.

A direction shaped together, with support before, during, and between sessions.

01

Understand the pattern

Explore the thoughts, rituals, avoidance, and reassurance that may be narrowing your day.

02

Choose a supported step

Work together on gradual practice that helps you respond differently to uncertainty.

03

Keep living your life

Reflect on what you learn between sessions and return to the things that matter to you.

A GOOD QUESTION

Will I have to face everything at once?

ERP is typically planned in gradual steps. You and your counsellor can discuss what the process would look like before beginning.
