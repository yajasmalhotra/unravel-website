When doubt starts deciding how you spend your day.

Checking once more. Replaying a conversation. Searching for an answer that finally feels certain. OCD can take hours without anyone else seeing what is happening. The difficulty is often less about the subject of a thought than the repeated demands that follow it.

UNDERSTANDING OCD

OCD is more than being careful or organised.

Obsessive-compulsive disorder can involve recurring obsessions, compulsions, or both. Obsessions are unwanted thoughts, images or urges. Compulsions are repetitive behaviours or mental acts that someone feels driven to perform, often to reduce distress or prevent a feared outcome. OCD symptoms can become time-consuming and interfere with ordinary activities. NIMH explains the symptoms and assessment of OCD.

Compulsions may be visible, such as washing or checking, or internal, such as mentally reviewing, repeating words or testing a feeling. Asking others for reassurance can also become part of the pattern. The brief relief may be followed by another question, another check or another attempt to settle the doubt.

Having an intrusive thought or a particular habit does not by itself establish OCD. Counselling considers the wider pattern, its purpose and its effect on your life. The guide to OCD themes and intrusive thoughts introduces common terms without asking you to diagnose yourself.

For a closer look at internal rituals, read mental compulsions in OCD.

ERP AND ACT

ERP addresses the cycle. ACT connects it with your life.

Exposure and Response Prevention is a form of cognitive behavioural therapy and a first-line psychological treatment for OCD. It pairs planned contact with triggers with practice changing the compulsive response. This includes attention to mental rituals and avoidance, not simply doing something that feels frightening. The IOCDF describes how ERP works.

Acceptance and Commitment Therapy is Unravel’s main therapeutic framework. In OCD care, ACT supports willingness, attention and action guided by your values alongside ERP. These approaches have different roles: ACT does not replace the OCD-specific response-prevention work.

For example, a goal might be spending an evening with family without losing most of it to mental checking. That goal gives practice a purpose and helps shape a plan suited to your needs, circumstances and readiness.

CULTURE & CONTEXT

Your beliefs and relationships are part of the context.

OCD may attach to things that matter deeply: responsibility, faith, relationships or being a good person. Understanding those commitments comes before deciding what a compulsive response looks like. A valued religious or cultural practice should not be treated as a symptom simply because it is unfamiliar. The IOCDF offers guidance on religiously sensitive ERP.

Unravel brings an understanding of South Asian and first-generation experiences while welcoming adults from all backgrounds. Shared identity does not mean identical experiences. Family expectations, privacy, belonging and the cost of asking for help deserve attention alongside the OCD pattern.

VIRTUAL OCD CARE

OCD counselling for Vancouver, delivered online.

Vancouver clients attend virtually, as do clients elsewhere in British Columbia. A private place to speak, a suitable device and a workable internet connection help make the online setting useful. Clients must be physically located in BC at the time of care.

Choosing an OCD therapist also means checking the person’s actual training and approach. Theekshitha’s completed ERP course and current designation are listed on the counsellor page. The IOCDF’s therapist-selection guide provides questions about OCD-specific methods, training and cultural context.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions about the approach.

Do I need a diagnosis before contacting Unravel?

No confirmed diagnosis is required to enquire. Describe what is happening and how it affects you. The consultation explores fit and next steps; it is not a formal diagnostic assessment or a diagnostic report.

Can OCD counselling address mental compulsions?

Yes. Mental reviewing, repeated internal phrases and other rituals can be relevant to the response-prevention plan. Their function and context matter; ordinary thinking is not automatically a compulsion.

Is OCD therapy available in person in Vancouver?

Unravel’s sessions are virtual. Adults in Vancouver and elsewhere in BC meet online and must be physically located in British Columbia during sessions.

How long does OCD treatment take?

There is no fixed course or outcome promised. Goals, session frequency, affordability and response to the work inform the plan and should be reviewed as counselling continues.
