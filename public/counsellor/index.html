Meet Theekshitha Vadladi.

Theekshitha Vadladi, a mental health counsellor, offers individual online counselling for OCD and anxiety in Vancouver and across BC. Her work brings together an ACT-led approach, OCD-specific ERP, and attention to cultural context.

Book a free consultation

A clear approach, with attention to your experience.

Therapy begins with understanding what your days are like: the thoughts that pull you in, the responses you feel driven to repeat, and the parts of life that are getting squeezed out. Theekshitha works collaboratively to connect that understanding with a practical direction.

As a South Asian woman and first-generation immigrant, she brings an understanding of navigating family expectations, belonging and change. She also recognises that a shared background never tells the whole story. Clients from all backgrounds are welcome.

ACT is her main therapeutic approach. ERP is used for OCD, including attention to mental rituals and reassurance-seeking. EMDR may be used for anxiety and trauma where appropriate. Each approach has a specific purpose within the work.

Understand the why

What is a response doing for you in the moment, and what is it costing over time? Those questions help guide the work.

Respect the context

Your values, faith, identity and responsibilities inform what a useful next step could be.

Review together

Questions, difficulties and the effect on everyday life help shape what needs attention next.

Completed professional training.

Acceptance & Commitment Therapy: 2-Day Intensive ACT Training

PESI · Daniel J. Moran, PhD, BCBA-D

Two-day training in ACT skills, case conceptualization and psychological flexibility. ACT is the main approach at Unravel.

4-Day Intensive Workshop: Exposure and Response Prevention Therapy for OCD

Cognitive Behavior Institute (CBI Center for Education) · Alissa Jerud, PhD

Four-day intensive training focused on Exposure and Response Prevention for OCD.

EMDR Basic Training

EMDR Training Associates · Alivia Maric and Eve Wilensky

Both parts of the EMDRIA-approved basic training and the required consultation are complete. The programme includes instruction, supervised practice and consultation.

Continued ACT reading supports learning alongside formal training. EMDRIA-approved basic training, including the required consultation, is complete; basic training and EMDRIA certification are distinct.

Training informs the approach, while your needs and the scope of the practice guide whether working together is appropriate. Ask about how ACT, ERP or EMDR would be used in your care.

Working together online.

Theekshitha works with adults aged 18 and over who are physically located in British Columbia during sessions, including Vancouver. All appointments are virtual.
