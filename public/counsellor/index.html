Meet Theekshitha Vadladi.

MY APPROACH

How I work with OCD and anxiety.

I want to understand what is bringing you to counselling, what you have already tried and what you would like to be able to do differently. That includes the pressures and responsibilities around you, as well as the thoughts, feelings and patterns we might work on.

I use ACT as my main framework. In OCD counselling, I use ERP for compulsions, including mental rituals and reassurance seeking. In anxiety counselling, we look at how worry and avoidance affect your choices. EMDR may be considered for anxiety and trauma where appropriate.

I’ll explain the reasons for the approach I suggest and ask how the work is affecting you. Read what the first appointments can involve.

CULTURE & IDENTITY

Culture, family and the changes you want.

As a South Asian woman and first-generation immigrant, I know that questions about family, belonging and change can carry a lot of history. I ask what culture, identity and relationships mean to you, rather than assuming our experiences are the same.

You may want to honour an important relationship or belief while changing an expectation that has become difficult to carry. We can make room for those conversations. People from all backgrounds are welcome.
