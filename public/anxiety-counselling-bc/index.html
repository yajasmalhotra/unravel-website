Anxiety counselling · virtual across BC

When your mind is always preparing for what could go wrong.

Anxiety can make rest, decisions, relationships, and even ordinary days feel like something to manage. Counselling can help you understand the pattern without treating you as the problem.

Book a consultation ↗

Anxiety often has a reason for being here.

You may be used to thinking ahead, reading the room, being prepared, and holding things together for everyone else. Those skills can be protective—and exhausting when your nervous system never gets the message that it is allowed to come down.

In therapy, we can get curious about the thoughts, body responses, expectations, and relationship patterns that keep anxiety in motion. The work may draw from ACT, trauma-informed, somatic, relational, and practical CBT-informed tools, always adapted to your pace and what matters to you.

Notice the pattern

Understand what anxiety is trying to prevent and the situations that keep it activated.

Build more room

Practise responding with greater choice, self-trust, and compassion instead of only urgency.

Bring your context

Family expectations, culture, work, identity, and relationship stress all belong in the conversation.
