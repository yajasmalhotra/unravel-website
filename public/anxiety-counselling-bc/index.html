When overthinking starts running the whole day.

You can know that you are safe and still feel your chest tighten, your thoughts race, or your mind rehearse every possible way something could go wrong.

Book a consultation

Anxiety is trying to protect you. It is also exhausting you.

Maybe you rewrite a simple message five times, replay conversations long after they end, or avoid decisions because every option feels risky. You might be the person everyone relies on while privately feeling one mistake away from everything falling apart.

Generic advice to “just stop worrying” is not the answer. Together, we will look at the thoughts, body responses, expectations, and relationship patterns keeping your nervous system on call. The work may draw from ACT, trauma-informed, somatic, relational, and practical CBT-informed tools, depending on what is actually useful for you.

Name the alarm

We will identify what your anxiety is trying to prevent, what sets it off, and what keeps the cycle going.

Stop treating every thought like an instruction

You can learn to respond to uncertainty without checking, rehearsing, or solving the same problem for the tenth time.

Keep the full context in the room

Family expectations, culture, work, identity, and relationship stress are not side notes. They are part of understanding why your anxiety makes sense.
