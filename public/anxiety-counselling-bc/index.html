Anxiety can be a signal, not a personal failure.

Anxiety can show up as racing thoughts, avoidance, restlessness, irritability, numbness, people-pleasing, perfectionism, or the feeling that you cannot rest even when you want to. It can make everyday decisions feel heavy and leave little room to notice what you need.

Counselling can help you understand the patterns around anxiety, make room for difficult internal experiences, and practise values-guided choices with more self-compassion. This is not about forcing yourself to “get over it”; it is about building a different relationship with what is happening.

Common concerns

Overthinking, uncertainty, emotional overwhelm, perfectionism, stress, and difficulty switching off.

Approach

Person-centred, ACT-informed, trauma-informed support that is adapted to your goals and pace.
