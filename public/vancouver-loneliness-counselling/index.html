Loneliness counselling in Vancouver

Surrounded by people. Still feeling alone?

Warm, culturally responsive virtual counselling for South Asian and first-generation immigrant women navigating the Vancouver Freeze and the ache of not feeling truly known.

Let’s unravel this togetherMeet Theekshitha

The Vancouver Freeze is real.

You may have a busy social calendar, kind coworkers, and a phone full of contacts, yet still struggle to form the deep friendships you want. In Vancouver, people can feel close enough to wave to but far enough away that you never quite get invited in. When you are also navigating a new city, racism, cultural differences, or the pressure to appear like you are thriving, loneliness can become deeply personal.

Therapy can help you separate “I am hard to know” from the conditions that have made connection difficult. Together, we can explore what happens when you reach out, the protective habits that keep you safe, and the kind of belonging you actually want. This is not about becoming more agreeable or performing confidence. It is about building self-trust, practising honest connection, and making room for relationships that feel reciprocal.

Support for cultural isolation

As a South Asian counsellor, I understand the experience of code-switching between corporate Vancouver, family expectations, and your private desires. You can bring the whole story here without explaining why community, dating, friendship, and family can feel complicated at the same time.

We may explore

Friendship, dating, social anxiety, belonging, self-worth, cultural isolation, and the fear of being a burden.

How I work

ACT, EMDR, and somatic and relational approaches, adapted to your pace and goals.

Where we meet

Secure virtual counselling for women located in British Columbia at the time of care.
