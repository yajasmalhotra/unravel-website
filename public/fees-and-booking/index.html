Fees and booking

A simple first step.

Unravel Counselling offers virtual sessions for adults located throughout British Columbia. Reach out to ask about current availability, fees, and whether a consultation would be a good place to begin.

Request a consultationRead common questions

What to include in your message

You are welcome to share a little about what you are looking for, whether you are located in British Columbia, and any questions you have about the process. You do not need to provide private clinical details in an initial email.

Current fee and availability information is shared directly so that it is accurate at the time you are considering care.

1. Reach out

Send an email to request a consultation or ask an initial question.

2. Connect

Use the consultation to discuss your hopes for counselling and practical details.

3. Begin, if it fits

If it feels right, you will receive the current next steps for virtual sessions.

Important note

Unravel Counselling is not a crisis or emergency service. If you are in immediate danger or need urgent support, call 911 or contact a local crisis service.
