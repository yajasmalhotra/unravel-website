Trauma therapy · virtual across BC

Make room for what happened, without being pulled back into it.

Trauma can shape how safe, close, alert, or exhausted life feels. Therapy offers a paced, collaborative space to understand what your mind and body have been carrying.

Book a consultation ↗

Nothing needs to be forced.

Trauma therapy is not about retelling every detail before you are ready. We begin by building enough steadiness and choice to notice what is present now: anxiety, shutdown, people-pleasing, anger, relationship patterns, or a body that never seems to get to rest.

Sessions are informed by trauma-sensitive, person-centred, ACT, somatic, relational, and EMDR approaches. The work is tailored to your goals and pace, with attention to safety and consent throughout.

At your pace

You decide what feels possible to explore. Therapy can move slowly, and pauses are part of the work.

Whole-context care

Your culture, family story, relationships, and present-day realities belong in the room.

Virtual across BC

Secure virtual sessions are available to adults located in British Columbia at the time of care.
