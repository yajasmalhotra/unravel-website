EMDR therapy · virtual across BC

When the past is still taking up space in the present.

EMDR therapy may be a helpful option when difficult experiences continue to affect how you feel, relate, or move through the world. It is always approached with preparation, collaboration, and choice.

Ask about EMDR ↗

EMDR is not a shortcut through your story.

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR) is a structured therapy approach that can be used to work with distressing memories and the beliefs, sensations, or responses connected to them. Whether it is a fit depends on what you are carrying, what support you have, and what feels safe enough to explore.

We start with the foundations: your goals, your current capacity, and ways to stay grounded. If EMDR is appropriate, it is integrated thoughtfully rather than rushed. A consultation is a good place to ask questions about the approach and decide together whether it makes sense for you.

Preparation first

Grounding, choice, and enough stability matter before any memory-focused work begins.

Collaborative care

You remain an active participant in decisions about the pace and direction of therapy.

Integrated approach

EMDR may sit alongside ACT, somatic, relational, and person-centred support.
