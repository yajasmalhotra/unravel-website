MORE ROOM TO LIVE.

A NEW RELATIONSHIP WITH THE PAST

Care that begins with your experience.

EMDR is a structured therapy that supports the processing of distressing memories. It can involve recalling parts of an experience while following alternating movements or sounds. Your counsellor can help you decide whether this approach fits what you want to work on.

At Unravel, therapy is a guided process. Time spent understanding your story and preparing for the work matters as much as the sessions themselves.

THE PROCESS

What care can look like.

A direction shaped together, with support before, during, and between sessions.

01

Find your bearings

Talk about what brings you here, what feels important, and what would help you feel ready.

02

Work at a considered pace

If EMDR is a fit, your counsellor guides the process and checks in with you as it unfolds.

03

Bring change into life

Make room to notice what shifts in daily life and what support you need between sessions.

A GOOD QUESTION

Do I need to know if EMDR is right for me?

No. A consultation is a place to talk about your goals and explore whether EMDR or another approach might be appropriate.
