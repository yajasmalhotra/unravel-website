Depression counselling · virtual across BC

When even the things you care about feel far away.

Depression can make life feel heavy, flat, disconnected, or hard to begin. Counselling offers a place to bring the parts of your experience that may be difficult to explain to anyone else.

Book a consultation ↗

You do not have to make it sound smaller.

Low mood, numbness, exhaustion, self-criticism, hopelessness, loss of motivation, or a sense of moving through life on autopilot can all change how you relate to yourself and others. You may still be functioning on the outside while feeling far away from yourself inside.

We begin with curiosity rather than pressure. Together, we can make sense of what has been weighing on you, notice the patterns that keep you isolated or depleted, and identify small, meaningful ways to reconnect with your needs, values, and support.

More than a label

Your experience is shaped by your relationships, culture, stress, history, and present circumstances.

Small, real steps

Therapy can make room for rest, self-trust, connection, and choices that feel possible right now.

Support, not crisis care

Unravel Counselling is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, call 911 or a local crisis service.
