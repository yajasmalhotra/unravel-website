When getting through the day takes everything you have.

You may still answer messages, show up for work, and take care of everyone else. That does not mean you are fine. Depression can hide behind a life that still looks functional.

Book a consultation

You can look capable and still feel completely checked out.

Maybe the plans you used to enjoy now feel like obligations. Small tasks take an absurd amount of effort. You cancel, withdraw, scroll, sleep, or keep moving on autopilot because stopping long enough to notice how you feel seems worse.

Recovery will not become another performance where you have to force gratitude or prove that you are trying hard enough. We will look honestly at what has been draining you, what keeps you isolated, and what kind of support is realistic right now. The goal is not to manufacture a better mood. It is to help you reconnect with yourself and your life in ways that feel possible.

Understand what has gone quiet

We will look beyond the label at your stress, relationships, culture, history, and the circumstances wearing you down.

Make the next step small enough

When motivation is low, an ambitious recovery plan becomes one more reason to feel behind. We will build from what you can actually do.

Know when you need more support

Unravel Counselling is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, call 911 or a local crisis service.
