MORE ROOM TO LIVE.

MOMENTS THAT FEEL LIKE YOURS

Care that begins with your experience.

Depression can affect energy, interest, connection, and the way the future feels. Counselling offers a place to understand your experience and consider what support could help you reconnect with daily life.

There is no single path forward. At Unravel, the work begins with your goals and can draw on ACT, EMDR, or other parts of a shared plan when appropriate.

THE PROCESS

What care can look like.

A direction shaped together, with support before, during, and between sessions.

01

Begin with your experience

Talk about what life has been like and what you would like more room for.

02

Shape the work together

Explore patterns, needs, and an approach that fits your circumstances.

03

Notice what returns

Make space for connection, meaning, and everyday moments as the work develops.

A GOOD QUESTION

Do I need to know what kind of therapy I want?

No. You can begin with a conversation about what you are experiencing and what you hope might change.
