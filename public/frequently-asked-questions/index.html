Frequently asked questions

Some things you may be wondering.

These answers cover the practical basics of virtual counselling with Unravel. A consultation is always the best place to ask questions specific to you.

Ask a questionFees and booking

Is virtual counselling available throughout BC?

Yes. Unravel Counselling offers virtual sessions to adults who are located in British Columbia at the time of care.

What do I need for a virtual session?

A private setting, a device with a camera and sound, and a reliable internet connection are helpful. Session details and the current video link are provided when you book.

What can I talk about in counselling?

People reach out for support with anxiety, emotional overwhelm, relationship patterns, identity, cultural stress, life transitions, self-worth, and many other concerns. You do not need to have everything figured out before beginning.

What is the approach?

The approach is warm, collaborative, person-centred, ACT-informed, and trauma-informed. The work is adapted to your goals, needs, and pace.

Are sessions available in other languages?

Sessions are available in English, Hindi, Telugu, and Tamil.

How do I know whether it is a good fit?

A consultation is a chance to ask questions, share what you are hoping for, and decide whether working together feels right.
