Therapy that makes room for cultural context.

Many South Asian and Indian clients look for therapy that understands how identity, family systems, migration, racialization, and community expectations can shape mental health. Theekshitha offers a warm space to explore anxiety, guilt, boundaries, self-trust, relationships, intergenerational expectations, and the pressure to keep functioning even when things feel heavy.

Therapy is not about choosing between independence and connection. It can be a place to understand what you carry, clarify your values, and move toward choices that feel honest while still honouring the complexity of where you come from.
