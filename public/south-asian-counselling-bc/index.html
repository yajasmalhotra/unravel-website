South Asian counselling in British Columbia

Therapy that makes room for cultural context.

Unravel Counselling provides virtual, culturally responsive counselling for South Asian adults throughout British Columbia. Sessions are available in English, Hindi, Telugu, and Tamil.

Book a consultationMeet Theekshitha

Your full context belongs in the room.

Identity, family systems, migration, racialization, faith, language, and community expectations can shape the way we understand responsibility, belonging, intimacy, and care. Therapy can be a space to speak about these experiences without needing to explain away their complexity.

You may be carrying anxiety, guilt, boundaries that feel difficult to set, the pressure to keep functioning, or questions about how to stay connected to yourself and the people you love. Counselling can make room to notice what matters to you and move toward choices that feel more honest.

Languages

English, Hindi, Telugu, and Tamil.

Common themes

Identity, cultural expectations, anxiety, relationships, intimacy, life transitions, and self-trust.

Format

Virtual counselling for adults located in British Columbia.
