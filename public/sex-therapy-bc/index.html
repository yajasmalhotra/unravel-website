Intimacy deserves room for honesty, curiosity, and care.

Sex therapy creates a nonjudgmental space to talk about desire, closeness, communication, shame, identity, cultural messages, and the ways life can affect intimacy.

There is no “normal” you need to perform.

Many people carry private questions about sex and intimacy for a long time—especially when culture, family, faith, gender expectations, relationship history, or past experiences have made those questions hard to name. You do not need to arrive with the right language.

We can explore the patterns, expectations, and conversations that shape your experience, individually or with a partner. The work is collaborative and affirming, with attention to your values, consent, and the kind of connection you want to build.
