Anxiety can be a signal, not a personal failure.

Anxiety often shows up as racing thoughts, avoidance, irritability, restlessness, numbness, people-pleasing, or a feeling that you cannot relax even when things are technically okay. Therapy can help you understand what anxiety is protecting, build emotional regulation, and move toward values-based choices with more self-compassion.

Theekshitha’s approach draws from Acceptance and Commitment Therapy and person-centered therapy, helping clients notice patterns, reconnect with their needs, and practice changes that feel realistic.
