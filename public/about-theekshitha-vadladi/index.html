About Unravel Counselling

Meet Theekshitha Vadladi.

Theekshitha is the founder and counsellor behind Unravel Counselling. She offers warm, collaborative virtual counselling for adults throughout British Columbia.

Book a consultationExplore counselling

A place to meet yourself with more honesty and less judgment.

Theekshitha works with people who are navigating anxiety, relationship patterns, self-worth, identity, emotional overwhelm, and experiences that can be difficult to name. Her approach is grounded in curiosity, collaboration, and the belief that people do not need to be fixed in order to be understood.

Together, counselling can make room for the parts of you that protect, please, avoid, or shut down—and for choices that feel more aligned with your values and your life.

Approach

Person-centred, ACT-informed, trauma-informed, and responsive to each client’s pace and goals.

Languages

Sessions are available in English, Hindi, Telugu, and Tamil.

Format

Virtual counselling for adults located anywhere in British Columbia.

Professional background

Unravel Counselling is built around thoughtful, ethical, and culturally responsive care. To help you decide whether working together feels right, a consultation is an opportunity to ask about approach, virtual sessions, availability, and the concerns you would like support with.
