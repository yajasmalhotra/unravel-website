About Theekshitha · Unravel Counselling

You should not have to explain your whole world before you feel understood.

I’m Theekshitha Vadladi, a South Asian counsellor supporting first-generation immigrant women across British Columbia. I know the feeling of being capable on the outside while quietly wondering who is holding you.

Let’s unravel this togetherExplore counselling

A counsellor who understands living between two worlds.

As a South Asian woman in my late 20s, I understand that cultural expectations are not a footnote. They can shape your relationship with success, rest, money, partnership, family, and even the word “no.” You may be the one who translates for your parents, keeps the peace, achieves the dream, and still feels lonely in Vancouver. You may love your family deeply and need more room to become yourself.

My work is warm, direct, and collaborative. You do not need to soften the truth or teach me why a boundary can feel like betrayal. We can make space for the guilt of success, the exhaustion of holding everyone else together, and the grief of not quite belonging anywhere.

ACT

Acceptance and Commitment Therapy helps you notice guilt and fear without letting them run your life, then choose actions connected to your values.

EMDR

EMDR may help process experiences that still live in your body, so old survival responses have less power in the present.

Somatic and relational work

We pay attention to your nervous system, relationships, and patterns so insight can become real relief—not another thing to perform.

Let’s see if this feels like a fit.

A consultation is a low-pressure first conversation. You can share what is bringing you here, ask about ACT, EMDR, or the way I work, and get a sense of whether you feel comfortable with me. You do not need the perfect words. Book a consultation and let’s unravel this together.
